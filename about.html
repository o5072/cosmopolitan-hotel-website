Our story

A hotel built around privacy, artistry, and the rhythm of the city.

Aurelia Grand brings together architectural calm, high-touch hospitality, and cultural access for guests who move through the world with intention.

Where modern luxury feels personal.

Set above a vibrant cultural district, the hotel was designed as a private vertical retreat. Every floor is tuned for quiet movement, from the scent in the arrival gallery to the low-glow corridors and panoramic lounges.

Our hosts curate stays around each guest: reservation timing, wellness preferences, dining mood, work rhythm, and the kind of city moments that never make it into guidebooks.
